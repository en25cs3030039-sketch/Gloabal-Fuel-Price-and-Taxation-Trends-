Analytical Objectives

What This Project Aims to Uncover

Energy markets are volatile, but retail prices paid by ordinary motorists and businesses reflect vastly different national priorities. This analytics project provides a rigorous multi-dimensional view using official Tableau Public visualizations.

1
Global Price Variance Mapping

Quantify price variance across geographic regions and evaluate the dispersion between oil-producing nations versus net fuel importers.

2
Taxation vs. Subsidy Regimes

Assess how tax percentages range from negative effective taxation (heavy sovereign subsidies) to over 50%+ ad-valorem excise duties.

3
Income Level Elasticity

Analyze how High, Upper-Middle, Lower-Middle, and Low Income economies structure their fuel pricing frameworks relative to citizen purchasing power.

4
Brent Crude Transmission

Measure how fluctuations in global Brent Crude benchmark prices pass through to end-consumer retail prices across fuels.

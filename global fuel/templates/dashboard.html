How to Interact with This Dashboard

1. Cross-Filtering Elements Click any country on the map or any bar on the breakdown chart to cross-filter other charts automatically.
2. Utilizing Dropdown Filters Use the Region and Income Level filters to isolate specific economic blocks (e.g. High Income OECD vs Sub-Saharan Africa).
3. Tooltip Inspections Hover your cursor over any data point to reveal underlying figures including Brent crude benchmark, taxes, and subsidies.
4. Resetting Selections Click on whitespace or use the reset/revert button on the Tableau bottom toolbar to clear all applied filters.
